# Job Application Email Prompt

## Prompt

Write a **short, professional, and specific job application email** based on the following structure:

### 1. Introduction

Mention my name, then use the **exact job title/role from the job description** as the position I am applying for.

- The role/title must change dynamically based on the job posting.
- Do **not** always use "Junior Full-Stack Developer."
- Also mention my **two main technologies that are most relevant to that specific job description**.
- Choose the technologies based on the requirements of the particular job posting.

**Example:**

If the job title is **Frontend Developer**, write:

> I'm Md Hasanuzzaman Shohag, a Frontend Developer with hands-on experience in React.js and Next.js.

If the job title is **React Developer**, write:

> I'm Md Hasanuzzaman Shohag, a React Developer with hands-on experience in React.js and TypeScript.

---

### 2. Why This Company

Briefly mention something positive about the company and explain why I am interested in joining.

- Make the reason relevant to the company or job.
- Avoid generic statements.
- Keep this section concise and natural.

---

### 3. Resume Attachment

Clearly mention that my **CV/resume is attached** for their consideration.

---

## Requirements

- Keep the email **short, professional, and specific**.
- Use natural and confident language.
- Avoid unnecessary details or generic statements.
- Use the **exact job title from the job description**.
- Select the **two most relevant technologies** based on the job requirements.
- Do **not** always use "Junior Full-Stack Developer" as my role.
- Do **not** include direct links to my portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, or other websites.
- Make the email suitable for sending directly to a company's **HR or Hiring Manager**.
- Include a professional **subject line**.
- Keep the email focused on the three sections above.
- Do not include my full skills list, contact information, or portfolio links unless specifically requested.

## Important Role Selection Rule

Always prioritize the **job title in the job posting** over my general/current professional title.

For example:

| Job Posting Title | Use This in the Introduction |
|---|---|
| Frontend Developer | Frontend Developer |
| Junior Frontend Developer | Junior Frontend Developer |
| React Developer | React Developer |
| Next.js Developer | Next.js Developer |
| Full-Stack Developer | Full-Stack Developer |
| MERN Stack Developer | MERN Stack Developer |
| Backend Developer | Backend Developer |

The introduction should always make it clear that I am applying for **that specific position**.

## Output Format

Return only the finished job application email with:

1. A professional subject line
2. Greeting
3. Short introduction
4. Short company-interest paragraph
5. Resume attachment paragraph
6. Professional closing

Do not add explanations, analysis, or extra sections outside the email.
